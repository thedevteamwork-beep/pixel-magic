## Final prompts

### Product

Use case: precise-object-edit / photographic asset extraction. The attached image is a UI mockup containing four photographs. Extract and faithfully reconstruct ONLY the FIRST, leftmost Product card: a transparent sculptural wavy glass perfume bottle filled with pale amber perfume, brushed gold cylindrical cap, on a rough cream travertine pedestal; warm late-afternoon Mediterranean light, softly blurred beige plaster background with dappled olive-leaf shadows. Preserve the reference bottle silhouette, cap proportions, stone texture, warm colors, camera position and centered composition. Deliver ONE standalone high-resolution photographic asset, landscape4:3 composition, edge-to-edge photography. Treat the card photograph as the edit target and the rest of the screenshot solely as context. Remove all UI, category labels, arrows, lettering, frame, rounded corners, page background and navigation. Reconstruct the small portions hidden by the category label with matching natural photo detail. Do not include any words, text, labels, logos or watermarks. Preserve the photograph rather than inventing a new concept; the asset must visibly match its card in the approved design.
